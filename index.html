In July the market panicked. The factories did not.
AI stock prices had a crash month that felt like 2022. Gavin Baker went looking for a real-world reason and could not find one. Chips were getting easier to get, rental prices were up, memory was up, and people were using more tokens, not less. The scare was in the price of the shares. It was not in the plants.
Force: the share price and the factory can point opposite ways. Read both.
From: Gavin Baker, Invest Like the Best, 4 Aug. MSM: FT Alphaville, BBC, and the FSB ran the July “AI bubble” scare. The talk peaked in the papers. The plants did not.

Compute is getting dearer, and old cards still earn
Useful intelligence is getting more expensive, not cheaper. An H100 is worth more today than three years ago. When a model got ten times cheaper, people used it thirteen times more. A six-year write-off looks wrong because the cards stay in the money long after the accountant is done with them.
Force: more demand for the same scarce machines, and the machines last.

6
Ordinary funding may be exhausted. That is why they are lending to themselves.
The plants and the labs need more cash than earnings, banks, and ordinary borrowing can cover. That claim still needs proof. If it is true, they have to invent other money: chip sellers lending to their own buyers, Nvidia reaching into insurance and pension money, the same dollars going around the same few companies and getting counted as revenue. Circular funding and the debt pile are what you do when the normal tap is dry. The BIS has already said the biggest cloud companies are spending faster than they earn. That is a start, not a close.
Force: if ordinary money is exhausted, the stack starts lending to itself. Prove the exhaustion, not the weirdness.
From: your frame. All-In (Gurley circular). Stratechery 11 Aug Nvidia’s risky business. Gavin Baker on credit wrappers. BIS on hyperscaler spend vs cash.

11 fight
Power is the problem. Nobody agrees what happens next.
Everyone serious now says intelligence will hit a wall made of electricity. That part is shared. The fight is about the future. One camp says China will add far more power than America next year, so energy stays the limit. Another camp says you can build power, and the real 2030 bind is the rarest machines that make the chips. A third camp says put the plants in space, because on the ground you cannot get a permit. These cannot all be right about what happens next.
Force: the problem is agreed. The path and the fix are not. The world will pick by what actually fails first.
From: Vishria 11 Aug. Dylan Patel 13 Mar. Dwarkesh Elon 5 Feb. Stratechery SpaceX IPO.

China is locking the ugly supply chains
China stopped exporting research monkeys. Prices jumped, US labs ran short. It already refines most rare earths and is moving to own the robots that run the mines, not just the rocks. In the Iran oil shock it quietly cut imports from stockpiles the West cannot see. The FCC just swept robot vacuums and humanoids onto a national-security import list.
